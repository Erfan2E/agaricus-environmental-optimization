# Research Roadmap

## Environmental Optimization in Agaricus bisporus Cultivation

### Project Overview

This project explores the application of metaheuristic optimization algorithms to environmental scheduling in *Agaricus bisporus* (button mushroom) cultivation.

The research is based on the study by Panayi et al. (2017), which investigates optimal environmental conditions for mushroom production using statistical modeling and mathematical optimization.

The project is organized into two main phases:

## Phase I — Understanding the Original Research

The first phase focuses on studying the original paper and understanding its methodology.

- Review the research objectives, assumptions, and methodology.
- Explore the dataset and identify its key variables.
- Study the VDFR statistical model and its role in the research.
- Investigate the original optimization method, including its objective function and constraints.
- Explore the possibility of reproducing the original model and results.

## Phase II — Metaheuristic Optimization

The second phase focuses on applying and evaluating alternative optimization algorithms.

- Reformulate the original optimization problem for metaheuristic algorithms.
- Investigate the Grey Wolf Optimizer (GWO) and select comparison algorithms.
- Implement the selected algorithms.
- Compare their performance with the original optimization method.
- Analyze solution quality, convergence, stability, and computational cost.

## Research Scope

The project focuses on computational modeling and optimization. Practical validation in commercial mushroom cultivation is outside the current scope.
